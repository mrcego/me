## Product Principles

1. **Evidence Over Assertion**: Every capability claim is backed by real production case studies, metrics, or live interactive demonstrations.
2. **Frictionless Conversion**: Clean, rapid access to contact forms, direct calendar booking, localized CV downloads, and role-specific hiring details in under two clicks.
3. **Engineering Craft as Design**: The portfolio itself is proof of architectural rigor — strict type safety, zero layout shifts, smooth 60fps animations, and strict security posture.
4. **AI as Leverage, Not Title**: AI tools accelerate delivery under senior human governance and rigorous quality gates.
